⚖️ The Legal Ruling (the critical audit result)

ITAR §120.33(b) explicitly excludes "general scientific, mathematical, or engineering principles commonly taught" and public-domain information. Skills describing process = general principles → publish-safe.
✅ Publish-safe: workflows, methodology, tool usage, certification planning, V&V, systems engineering — general engineering principles
⛔ Publish-gated: defense-article-specific data (dimensions, tolerances, part numbers, USML-class performance parameters) — ITAR/EAR-controlled regardless of packaging
✅ Standards: DO-178C/DO-254/ARP4754A/AS9100 can be referenced and summarized — never quoted wholesale (RTCA/SAE/IAQG copyright)
✅ EU/Estonian: Regulation 2021/821 dual-use — methodology is fine; ITAR-free architecture is a procurement advantage in Europe

CEO verdict: AeroSkills can publish safely with a clear content policy + Apache-2.0 + compliance banner. The legal gate is a content rule, not a blocker.

🛠️ Tool Licensing — the other ruling

A skill is text/instructions, not a derivative work — AeroSkills can legally reference and script every tool, GPL included. Never vendor tool source/binaries into the library.
