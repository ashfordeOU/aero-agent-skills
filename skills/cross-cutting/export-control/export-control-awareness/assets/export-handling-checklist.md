# Export Handling Checklist

Operational checklist for aerospace engineering releases. Complete one
row per release event: email to a foreign collaborator, shared drive
access, conference presentation, publication submission, vendor data
package, or hiring a foreign national onto a project.

## Before any release

- [ ] Item or data set described in one free-text string.
- [ ] Topic screened with flag_restricted_topic(); all red flags
      recorded in the release note.
- [ ] Information basis established: published, textbook, patent,
      conference, fundamental-research, or unpublished.
- [ ] For fundamental research: confirm no agreement restricts
      publication or sharing (no side letter, no pre-publication review
      clause). Record the agreement reference.
- [ ] Decision tree run with the real audience and purpose; verdict,
      jurisdiction, and risk recorded.
- [ ] Recipient identified: US person, foreign person, or public.
      For a foreign person, note their country of nationality and check
      whether a deemed export applies.
- [ ] Verdict confirmed by the trade compliance office. An engineer's
      own assessment is not an authorization.

## Release rules

- [ ] No controlled data exported, deemed exported, or reexported
      without a license or a valid license exception.
- [ ] No email, attachment, shared drive, or remote access to controlled
      data for a foreign person without prior authorization.
- [ ] No conference or publication submission of controlled data
      without prior authorization.
- [ ] Nothing marked or represented as compliant, certified, or
      authorized except by the compliance office.
- [ ] No part numbers, procurement references, or supplier data
      appended to a release note beyond what the compliance office
      approved.

## After the release

- [ ] Classification basis, recipient, and license exception (if any)
      recorded and retained.
- [ ] Release note filed with the compliance office.
- [ ] Screening re-run whenever item, audience, or purpose changes; a
      verdict is only valid for the inputs it was computed from.
- [ ] If in doubt at any point, treat the data as controlled and stop
      the release until the compliance office responds.

## Red flags that stop the release

- [ ] Any red-flag topic match (turbine blade alloys, propulsion,
      missiles and rockets, sensors, avionics, low-observable design,
      spacecraft, unmanned aerial systems, radiation-hardened
      electronics, cryptographic items, hypersonics, directed energy,
      specialty composites, energetic materials, fire control, military
      aircraft and engines, defense services).
- [ ] Foreign person in the loop and the data is not confirmed public
      domain.
- [ ] Research agreement with publication or sharing restrictions.
- [ ] Item described by the USML or by a 600-series ECCN.
