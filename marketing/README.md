# Marketing Department

**Purpose:** how we're seen: positioning, content, GTM.

## Workspace
- `positioning-1pager.md`: positioning and messaging, draft-in-tree and founder-gated
- `release-notes-v1.0.0-rc1.md`: v1.0.0-rc1 release notes, draft-in-tree and founder-gated

## Operating rules
1. Founder-guided: no publish without explicit GO
2. Grounded: every claim traceable to the product/research
3. Anti-slop: no AI-tells, real voice
4. Proof before promise: show the artifact, not the idea

## Inputs
- Research market signals → positioning
- Development builds → demos

## Outputs
- GTM plan → founder GO → launch
