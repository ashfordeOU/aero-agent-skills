# Finance Department

**Purpose:** how we survive — costs, pricing, runway, invoices.

## Workspace
- `ledger/` — costs, spend, commitments
- `pricing/` — pricing models + research
- `invoices/` — billing records

## Operating rules
1. Every spend recorded; >€50 = founder VETO domain
2. Pricing grounded in research, not guesses
3. Numbers verified — never estimated without a label

## Inputs
- Ops cost data, research pricing signals

## Outputs
- Cost reports, pricing recommendations, runway view
